Discbreaker privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026. Draft, not yet in force.

Discbreaker is made by Numerals, a studio in Montréal, Québec, Canada. This policy covers the gameplay data the game itself sends us. It is short on purpose. For anything it doesn't answer, write to support@numerals.io.

In short

  • The full game sends us nothing.
  • The demo sends anonymous gameplay stats only if you choose SHARE. You can turn it off any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • The playtest sends anonymous gameplay stats. Sharing them is a condition of joining the playtest; the game tells you before you play, and you can quit instead.
  • We never receive your name, your Steam ID or anything you type.

What we collect

When sharing is on, the game sends:

  • A random ID made by the game on your computer. It isn't linked to your Steam account, and you can reset it in Settings → Privacy.
  • About the build and the session: the game version, playtest or demo, your operating system family (Windows or Linux), your language setting, screen size range, whether you use a keyboard or a controller, and how long you played.
  • How you play: when a run starts and ends and how far it went, the cards you were offered and the ones you took, what you bought in the shop, match results (score, overtime, duration), the campaign seed, and, on the playtest, how online cup matches ended.
  • Crashes: that the game closed unexpectedly, with the last few error lines (file paths removed).

What we never collect

Your name, Steam ID or Steam friends, your club's or players' names, chat, anything you type, your IP address (our analytics provider discards it), or your hardware's serial numbers.

Why

To balance the game and fix bugs: which cards are too strong, where runs end, what crashes. We don't sell this data, use it for advertising, or combine it with any other data about you.

Who handles it

The data is stored by PostHog, our analytics provider, in its European Union cloud. They process it for us and for nothing else.

How long we keep it

Up to 12 months, then it is deleted.

Legal basis

  • Playtest: our legitimate interest in testing the game, which is the purpose of the playtest you chose to join.
  • Demo: your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings → Privacy at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the data linked to your ID. Write to support@numerals.io with the ID shown in Settings → Privacy. Resetting your ID there also stops anything new from being linked to the old one.

If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec, or, in the European Union, to your country's data protection authority.

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: the studio's founder, at support@numerals.io.

Steam

Steam is run by Valve. What Steam collects is covered by Valve's own privacy policy, not this one. Discbreaker's gameplay data never includes your Steam data.

Children

Discbreaker isn't aimed at children under 13, and the data above doesn't identify anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date above changes, and the game shows its notice or question again before sending anything.
