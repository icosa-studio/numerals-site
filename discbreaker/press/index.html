CREATE YOUR CLUB

Every run is played with a club of your own: its name, two colours, a crest built from a logo and lettering, and its kits. The crest goes on the pitch, the scoreboard and every card.

  • Name, colours, crest, lettering
  • Home and inverted kits
  • Change it any time, for free

BUILD A SQUAD

Every run starts with tickets and an empty dressing room. Buy packs, open them, sign players and kit them out before the first whistle.

  • Ten pack shapes: the better the grade, the smaller the pack
  • A ticket allowance, so no two squads match
  • Formations set how your five line up

BEND THE RULES

Ghost passes slip through defenders. Tackles pop the disc toward their net. Stack sponsors on the crest and gear on your players until the match plays your way.

  • Up to six sponsors, each a rule for the whole side
  • Gear on every player
  • A shop after every win: reroll or buy packs

NO TWO RUNS ALIKE

A new 256-team bracket every run, and one loss ends it. Fame opens dynasty perks, and the veterans you keep bring their traits into the next run.

  • Eight knockout rounds to the final
  • Veterans carry over; traits come only from them
  • Dynasty perks: a bigger purse, more veterans

UP TO 256 PLAYERS

Friend cups: host an online tournament for your friends or your whole community. Everyone builds a squad on the spot, by draft or from sealed packs, then it's a knockout to the final. Up to 256 players in one cup.

FEATURES

  • Create your own club: name, colours, crest, kits
